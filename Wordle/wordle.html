How to play and get help

Guess a five-letter word in six tries. Green with ✓ means correct spot. Yellow with ~ means the letter belongs somewhere else. Gray with × means the letter is not in the word.

Use your keyboard or the on-screen keys. Press Enter to submit and Backspace to erase. Short guesses are not submitted, and there is no timer or penalty.

Your past guesses stay visible. You can open this Help section any time.
